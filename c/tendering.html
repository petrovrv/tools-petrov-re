Tendering & Contracts

6 tools for tendering and contracts for client, contractor and equipment supplier: procurement strategy and procedure, award criteria and price scoring, commercial bid normalisation and life-cycle cost, abnormally low tenders, bid/no-bid, estimates, quote levelling, Monte Carlo contingency, back-to-back terms, contract risk, cash flow, price indexation, liquidated damages and bonds, supplier compliance, lead time and conformity.

  • Award Criteria & Price Scoring DesignerWhich criteria, weights and price formula pick the best bid without rank reversal? Compares eight price-scoring methods on the same bids, shows live when adding or removing a bidder flips the ranking, prices one quality point in euro, maps the winner against the price weight and recommends a formula with its reasons.Benchmark tested · 243/243 · Stages: Procurement · Benchmark reportEN UA DE NL FR ES PL
  • Commercial Bid NormalisationWhat does each bid really cost, apples to apples? Arithmetic check of the price schedule (KB 18.04.2017 art. 34), missing prices filled by the art. 86 formula, options per art. 87, rebates, currency, delivery-term (Incoterms 2020) gaps and payment terms as present value, with a ranked table for the award.Benchmark tested · 350/350 · Stages: Procurement · Benchmark reportEN UA DE NL FR ES PL
  • Abnormally Low Tender CheckWhich bids must be asked to justify their price, and is the justification credible? Belgian art. 36 §4 check (15 % below the trimmed mean) with its conditions and exemptions, statistical screens, unit-price check per line against the median, and the justification checklist with the outcome and notifications.Benchmark tested · 415/415 · Stages: Procurement · Benchmark reportEN UA DE NL FR ES PL
  • Bid / No-Bid DecisionIs this tender worth the bid cost? Rolls back the decision tree of one or two gates (qualification, then tender) to the expected value of bidding, gives the breakeven probability of award against the base rate 1/N and your hit rate, counts margin erosion, the opportunity cost of a fully booked team and a stated strategic value, scores the fit, checks knock-outs and shows what moves the answer.Benchmark tested · 241/241 · Stages: Procurement · Benchmark reportEN UA DE NL FR ES PL
  • Subcontractor & Supplier Quote LevellingWhich quote is really cheapest? Fills every quote's scope gaps with plugs (own estimate, or the highest or mean price the other bidders gave), adds priced options, lands each quote on site in euro by its Incoterms 2020 rule with duty on the customs value, prices advances and stated commercial differences, ranks on paper and levelled, and shows how far your plugs may be wrong before the winner changes.Benchmark tested · 203/203 · Stages: Procurement · Benchmark reportEN UA DE NL FR ES PL
  • Cost & Schedule Risk (Monte Carlo)How much contingency for P50 / P80, and which risks drive it? Simulates three-point cost ranges, risk events and a schedule of parallel paths together, with rank correlation, time-dependent cost and capped delay damages; gives the confidence of the base estimate, the contingency at any P, the S-curves, the joint cost-schedule confidence and a tornado of the drivers.Benchmark tested · 57/57 · Stages: Basic engineering, Procurement · Benchmark reportEN UA DE NL FR ES PL
