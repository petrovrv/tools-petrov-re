MEP Maintenance & Operations

6 tools for maintenance of building services (HVAC, plumbing, fire protection, electrical and controls): asset criticality, risk-based and reliability-centred maintenance strategy, condition monitoring, maintenance planning and budgets, critical spares and renewal planning.

  • MEP Asset CriticalityRanks building services assets A/B/C from safety, environment, service and cost consequence, verified redundancy, recovery against tolerable downtime and condition evidence.Benchmark tested · 91/91 · Stages: Handover, Operation & maintenance · Benchmark reportEN UA DE NL FR ES PL
  • MEP Maintenance Strategy (RCM)Reliability-centred maintenance for building services: per failure mode the RCM decision diagram picks condition monitoring, scheduled replacement, a failure-finding test, run-to-failure or redesign, with the interval and the reason.Benchmark tested · 150/150 · Stages: Handover, Operation & maintenance · Benchmark reportEN UA DE NL FR ES PL
  • Condition Monitoring AdvisorWhat to measure, how often, and does it pay? Usable P–F interval per technique, inspection interval, probability of catching the failure and cost-benefit ranges with the break-even downtime cost.Benchmark tested · 65/65 · Stages: Operation & maintenance · Benchmark reportEN UA DE NL FR ES PL
  • MEP Maintenance PlannerTurns maintenance tasks into a 52/53-week calendar: hours per trade and week, season and shutdown windows, visit grouping, levelling within each task's tolerance, conflicts, budget and a CMMS work-order CSV.Benchmark tested · 25/25 · Stages: Handover, Operation & maintenance · Benchmark reportEN UA DE NL FR ES PL
  • Critical Spares & RecoveryWhich spares to hold, share, contract or rent? Expected annual cost of no stock, k spares on site (Poisson demand over the lead time, service level), a pool shared between sites, a supplier agreement and temporary plant, with the recovery time of each option.Benchmark tested · 74/74 · Stages: Handover, Operation & maintenance · Benchmark reportEN UA DE NL FR ES PL
  • MEP Renewal PlannerWhen to renew each building services asset: renewal year from condition (not age), criticality, refrigerant rules and support end; 25-year capex profile under a budget cap with the risk added by deferral.Benchmark tested · 77/77 · Stages: Operation & maintenance · Benchmark reportEN UA DE NL FR ES PL
