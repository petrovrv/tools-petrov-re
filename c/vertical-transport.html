Buildings / Vertical Transport

3 tools for vertical transport in buildings: lift (elevator) passenger waiting times and group control, round-trip-time traffic design and sizing, zoning of tall buildings and lift energy and efficiency class.

  • Lift Waiting Time SimulatorAnimated, deterministic simulation of a lift group: how long people really wait (mean, 95th percentile, share over 60 s) and what changes it — call buttons vs destination keypads, dispatch rules, zoning, parking, habits, one more car or faster doors — with an A/B comparison on the same passengers.Benchmark tested · 30/30 · Stages: Concept, Basic engineering, Detailed engineering, Operation & maintenance · Benchmark reportEN UA DE NL FR ES PL
  • Lift Traffic Design (round-trip time)Classical up-peak lift design: round-trip time, interval, 5-minute handling capacity, %POP and an average waiting time estimate against criteria for the building use; sizing grid of cars × load × speed, zoning of tall buildings with express runs, and why one-floor trips never reach rated speed.Benchmark tested · 245/245 · Stages: Concept, Basic engineering, Detailed engineering · Benchmark reportEN UA DE NL FR ES PL
  • Lift Energy & Efficiency ClassDaily and annual energy of one lift by the ISO 25745-2:2015 method — running plus idle and standby — with VDI 4707 classes, cost, CO₂ and a waterfall of what helps: regenerative drive, deep standby, LED car light.Benchmark tested · 45/45 · Stages: Basic engineering, Detailed engineering, Operation & maintenance · Benchmark reportEN UA DE NL FR ES PL
