Evaluated / custom FPY import

Import contract. Use dimensionless yield fraction per fission (0.06 = 6%), with optional 1σ uncertainty in the same units. Independent yields may seed the decay inventory. General cumulative evaluated yields are display-only here because seeding every cumulative product would double-count precursor feeding. In ENDF-6 terminology, MT 454 is independent FPY and MT 459 is cumulative FPY.

Fission irradiation · thin target

Boundary. Constant flux and one-group σ; target depletion includes fission, capture and radioactive decay. Product decay during irradiation is solved by CRAM48. Neutron reactions on fission products, self-shielding and spatial flux depression are omitted. Production-matrix transfer requires independent FPY.
